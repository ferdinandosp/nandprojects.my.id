About

Built for people, not the cloud

Nandprojects is a solo-founder studio using AI-assisted development to design, build, and ship offline-first mobile apps and tools. Every app stores data locally on your device first — no account required, no constant internet connection needed. When you want the convenience of cloud backup, apps offer an optional backup & restore feature powered by your own Google Drive account. Your data never touches Nandprojects servers.
