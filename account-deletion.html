Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: 2026-08-19

Some Nandprojects apps allow you to create an account (via Google Sign-In) to enable optional backup and restore of your app data to your personal Google Drive. If you would like to delete your account and any associated data, follow the steps below.

What Gets Deleted

  • Account record — the record of your Google account email address stored within the app will be removed.
  • Backup data on Google Drive (via Nandprojects) — any app data that was backed up to your Google Drive by the app on your behalf will be deleted from your Drive if Nandprojects still has OAuth access to perform this action.

Note: Data stored only locally on your device is not transmitted to Nandprojects and cannot be deleted remotely. To remove it, uninstall the app or clear app data through your device's Settings. Data in your Google Drive that the app can no longer access can be deleted by you directly — open Google Drive, find the folder named after the app, and delete it.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Send an email to ferdinando.satria@gmail.com with the subject line: Account Deletion Request.
  2. In the body of the email, include:
    • The email address associated with your account.
    • The name of the Nandprojects app you are using.

Your request will be processed within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation email once deletion is complete.

Questions

If you have any questions about the deletion process, please contact: ferdinando.satria@gmail.com
